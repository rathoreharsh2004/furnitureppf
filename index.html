What is Furniture PPF?

Protection that lets your furniture stay visible.

Furniture protection film is a transparent protective layer applied to suitable surfaces to help reduce everyday surface damage while preserving the appearance of the furniture underneath.

From statement dining tables and luxury sofas to cabinets and premium interior surfaces, the goal is simple: protect the finish without covering up the design you paid for.

FAQ

Questions before you protect?

Suitability depends on the material, finish and condition. Common applications can include suitable wood, marble, stone, glass, finished panels and selected furniture surfaces.

Suitable transparent films are designed to keep the original visual character visible, but the exact appearance depends on the surface and film selected.

Upholstery requires a surface-specific protection approach. Some fabric and leather furniture may be better suited to protective coatings rather than a conventional rigid surface film.
